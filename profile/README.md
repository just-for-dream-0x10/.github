## 🌟 About Us

**JustForDream** is an open-source organization dedicated to exploring the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence** and **Music**. We believe that technology can empower creativity, preserve cultural heritage, and make music more accessible to everyone.

Our team consists of passionate developers, researchers, and music enthusiasts who share a common dream: to build intelligent tools that understand, analyze, and create music.

---

## 🚀 Our Projects

### 🎻 [InstrumentTimbre](https://github.com/just-for-dream-0x10/InstrumentTimbre)
> AI-Powered Chinese Instrument Recognition

A production-ready AI system that automatically identifies traditional Chinese musical instruments from audio recordings with **97.86% accuracy**.

**Highlights:**
- 🎯 97.86% validation accuracy
- 🎭 Supports Erhu, Pipa, Guzheng, Dizi, Guqin, and more
- 📊 34 acoustic features optimized for Chinese music
- 🖼️ Professional visualizations and analysis charts

---

### 🛠️ [MusicTools](https://github.com/just-for-dream-0x10/MusicTools)
> Comprehensive Music AI Processing Toolkit

A versatile toolkit offering audio processing, format conversion, music analysis, and AI-powered features.

**Highlights:**
- 🔄 Audio format conversion (WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG)
- 🎼 Audio to MIDI conversion
- 🎤 Audio separation (vocals, drums, bass, etc.)
- 📈 15 types of audio visualizations
- 🔊 Audio restoration and enhancement

---

### 🔬 [Model Explorer](https://github.com/just-for-dream-0x10/Model_explorer)
> Neural Network Computation Dissection Table

A Streamlit-based interactive tool that dissects every layer of neural networks — parameters, FLOPs, and numerical computation processes. **~25,000 lines of code, 18 functional modules.**

**Highlights:**
- 🔢 Parameter & FLOPs calculator for 8 layer types and 7 classic networks (ResNet, VGG, BERT, GPT-2, ViT...)
- 🖼️ CNN, GNN, RNN/LSTM, ViT, MoE, ResNet — full architecture breakdown
- 🔬 Numerical stability detection (gradient vanishing/explosion diagnosis)
- 🧬 Single neuron analysis and architecture comparison lab

---

### 🏗️ [Transformer Explorer](https://github.com/just-for-dream-0x10/Transformer_explorer)
> Transformer & Mamba Architecture Visualizer

A math-principle-driven visualization tool for understanding Transformer and Mamba (SSM) architectures through Manim animations and interactive experiments.

**Highlights:**
- 🎬 Manim animations for Encoder/Decoder, Multi-Head Attention, RoPE, SwiGLU
- 🐍 Mamba selective mechanism and discretization process visualization
- 🧪 Interactive parameter lab — tune embedding dim, heads, state dim, temperature
- ⚡ Transformer vs Mamba complexity comparison and optimizer profiling
